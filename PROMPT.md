# Undivided One research charter

## Frozen objective

Build a durable, auditable two-phase program that strengthens the public Dutch and English Undivided One material without silently shifting its theology.

1. **Initial correction:** investigate identified weak claims and methodological overreach; submit substantive canonical changes as human-reviewable proposals.
2. **Expansion:** move detailed exegesis, word studies, historical excursions, objections, and bibliographies into subessays while compressing the main essay.

## Thesis under test

Preserve as a hypothesis—not an untouchable conclusion—that God is one and indivisible; Jesus is neither a second god nor merely human; Israel's God is genuinely known and made visible in Jesus; the Spirit is God's own active presence; and later models must be tested against explicit scriptural data.

## Canon and evidence

- The live site is the current public baseline. Record route and access date before every content task.
- The repository is the implementation and version-history environment.
- Log discrepancies; never resolve them silently.
- The site is not evidence for its own claims. Prefer primary-language texts, critical editions, lexica, peer-reviewed research, and recognized monographs.

## Boundaries

- Distinguish observation, non-observation, interpretation, weighting, and conclusion.
- State genre, direct context, historical frame, alternatives, uncertainty, and confidence.
- Steelman relevant alternatives and apply equal evidential standards.
- Do not use AI summaries, anonymous blogs, unverifiable quotations, or long copyrighted extracts as load-bearing evidence.
- Never auto-publish, auto-merge, or auto-deploy.
- Do not alter canonical theological content until its individual proposal is explicitly approved.

## Definition of ready for core revision

ECHAD-002 through ECHAD-009 are `review` or `done`, the live baseline has been rechecked, every retained claim has evidence status, and a human has approved the relevant proposals.

## Definition of done

The eventual NL core essay is approximately 25–35% shorter than its recorded baseline (12,644 rendered words on 2026-07-12), never longer without a documented decision; EN mirrors approved NL meaning; relocated detail remains reachable as sourced subessays; validation passes; and every canonical change traces to an approved proposal.
