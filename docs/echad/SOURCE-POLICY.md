# Source policy

## Canonical roles

- **Live site:** public visitor baseline; record exact route, page title, and access date.
- **Repository:** implementation, version history, tests, and unpublished work.
- **External evidence:** support for theological, linguistic, historical, and textual claims. The live site cannot support itself.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Primary Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek texts; critical editions and apparatus.
2. Standard lexica and grammars.
3. Peer-reviewed research and recognized academic monographs/commentaries.
4. Transparent scholarly translations and institutional reference works.

Record full bibliography, DOI/URL where available, access date, passages/entries consulted, and access limitations. Keep quotations short; paraphrase carefully. AI summaries, anonymous blogs, partisan apologetic pages, and unattributed quotations cannot carry an argument. Apply one standard to all models.

## Claim evidence labels

- `supported`: explicit evidence and serious alternatives assessed.
- `qualified`: core direction plausible but wording/extent exceeds evidence.
- `contested`: serious alternatives remain and discriminating evidence is limited.
- `unsupported`: asserted evidence does not establish the claim.
- `unreviewed`: not yet audited.
