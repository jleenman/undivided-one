# Methodology

For each text, keep these sections separate:

1. **Observation:** wording, morphology, grammar, syntax, textual variants, discourse placement, genre, direct context, and authorial perspective.
2. **Not explicit:** conclusions or categories the passage does not itself state.
3. **Interpretations:** at minimum the strongest relevant alternative; for model comparison include trinitarian, unitarian, Oneness/modalist, and identity-based options fairly.
4. **Weighting:** which explicit data discriminate among readings; do not let a system become a lexical gloss.
5. **Provisional conclusion:** bounded claim, counterevidence, revision criterion, and confidence `hoog`, `middel`, or `laag`.

Keep textual tension visible. Do not infer ontology merely from grammar, audience reaction, metaphor, spatial imagery, or later terminology. Identify modern analytical terms as tools rather than biblical vocabulary.
