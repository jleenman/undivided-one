# Source policy

Use this priority order:

1. Primary Hebrew/Aramaic/Greek text and critical editions; document edition and apparatus consulted.
2. Authoritative lexica and grammars.
3. Peer-reviewed articles and recognized academic monographs/commentaries.
4. Transparent scholarly translations and institutional reference works.

Record author/editor, title, edition or journal/volume/pages, publisher/year, DOI/URL when available, and access date. Distinguish consulted evidence from further reading. Do not use the live site, AI output, anonymous blogs, apologetic summaries, or unattributed quotations as load-bearing support. Paraphrase copyrighted prose and quote only the minimum primary text needed.

If a paywall prevents verification, cite metadata only and do not claim to have read the full work. If primary evidence or a critical apparatus is unavailable, lower confidence or block the claim.
